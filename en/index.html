Privacy Policy — DeMotion

Last updated: 17 November 2026. This policy concerns DeMotion for iOS. The controller is Maciej Siemiński; contact mail@macsiem.dev.

Photos and local data

DeMotion converts Live Photos on your device using PhotoKit. It creates a replacement still image and requests deletion of the original in the same PhotoKit transaction; the replacement is a new asset. Apple Photos and iCloud may synchronize or retain items according to your system settings. DeMotion does not upload photo content to its servers. App preferences and conversion history are stored on the device.

Optional update notifications

Update notifications are off until you choose to enable them and allow notifications in iOS. To deliver updates, our Cloudflare Workers service stores a random installation device ID, a hash of an ownership token, an Expo push token, platform, app version and build, locale, premium status, release channel and the selected updates category in Workers KV and Durable Objects. Expo and Apple Push Notification service (APNs) deliver messages. Disabling updates requests removal of the registration; if offline, the app retries on a subsequent foreground launch with connectivity. You can also control notification permission in iOS Settings. Uninstalling the app may not immediately remove a server registration; contact us to request deletion. The ownership hash is retained separately after disabling updates.

Version information

Independently of notification permission, the app reports a random installation ID, platform, app version and build, and release channel to the Cloudflare service to understand deployed versions. A hash of an ownership token is stored separately. The version record in Workers KV expires 90 days after its latest report; the ownership hash is retained separately. Contact us to request deletion.

Crash diagnostics

Sentry receives restricted technical crash and error reports for stability: an event ID, timestamps, exception type, selected stack and debugging information, platform, release, build, release channel and source-code revision (source SHA). The filter removes free-form error messages, user data, photo content and identifiers, breadcrumbs, and attachments; performance tracing, replay and screenshots are disabled. Network service providers may process connection information. Reports are kept only as long as needed for diagnostics, subject to applicable obligations; Sentry’s policy below provides further information. The configured period and processing location have not been specified here.

Advertising and purchases

The free version uses Unity Ads. Unity may process device and advertising identifiers where available, network and ad interaction data under its privacy policy. On iOS, the system App Tracking Transparency (ATT) choice controls permission for tracking as defined by Apple; The app communicates advertising privacy settings to Unity. ATT permission and consent required by data protection law are distinct. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings. Apple handles in-app purchases and payment information; DeMotion receives purchase entitlement information, not your payment card number. On iOS, the app sends Unity settings indicating no consent to personalized advertising, regardless of the ATT choice.

Purposes and legal bases

We process local data to provide the conversion you request; optional notifications on your request and consent; version information and crash diagnostics for our legitimate interest in understanding deployed versions and diagnosing faults; and purchase information to deliver purchases. Advertising processing uses the applicable legal basis and permissions required by law. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Recipients, retention and transfers

Service providers include Cloudflare (notification infrastructure), Expo and Apple (notification delivery), Sentry (crash reporting), Unity (advertising) and Apple (purchases). Providers may process data in other countries, subject to applicable law. Notification registration is retained while enabled and removed on a successful disable request; local data remains until deleted or the app is removed, subject to system backups. Other providers retain data according to their configurations and policies. Contact us for details of retention and international transfer safeguards.

Your rights and contact

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing. You may complain to your data protection authority; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO). Contact mail@macsiem.dev. We may need information to locate a record or verify a request. You can manage photo, notification and tracking permissions in iOS Settings.

Contact and changes

If you contact us, we process the information you send to answer your request; optional diagnostic details are included only if you choose them. We may update this policy and will publish the updated date here.
